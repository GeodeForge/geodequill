Privacy policy

Short, because there's little to tell. Last updated 3 November 2026.

This site collects nothing about you

No analytics script, no cookie, no tracking pixel, and no third-party request of any kind. The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles each request your browser makes, including your IP address, and its statistics show us totals: roughly how many people arrived and from where. We can't tell one visitor from another. Cloudflare keeps its own records under its privacy policy.

GeodeQuill sends nothing to us

GeodeQuill runs inside the AI app you use. The skill and plugin you install have no server and no account, and make no connection of their own. Your voices are kept where you put them: a folder on your computer, or text you save yourself. We never receive them.

Don't take our word for it. The source is on GitHub. The checker is one Python file, and you can read every line.

The check page and the Make my voice page run in your browser

On the check page and the Make my voice page, the checker runs inside your browser. What you paste there is never sent anywhere, and the pages load their files only from this site. A voice you make there is kept only if you save or copy it. If you choose to try it on the check page, your browser hands it from one page to the other inside the same tab, and the check page clears it from that hand-off as soon as it reads it.

GeodeQuill's ChatGPT app

In ChatGPT, GeodeQuill can run as an app that ChatGPT connects to at quill.geodeforge.com. When it does, ChatGPT's servers ask ours for GeodeQuill's instructions, a built-in voice or the list of tones, and we send that fixed text back. The app has no way to receive your writing: its tools take only the name of a set of instructions or a voice. It stores nothing and keeps no logs. Cloudflare runs it, under its privacy policy.

Your AI app does see your writing

Whatever you give ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI app goes to the company that runs it, under that company's terms. GeodeQuill can't change that, so it's worth reading their privacy terms before you hand over anything sensitive.

If you email us

  • What we receive. If you write to quill@geodeforge.com, we receive your message and your email address.
  • Why. A person reads it, and we use it only to answer you and fix what you found. We never sell it, share it, or add you to a mailing list.
  • Who handles it. Cloudflare forwards mail sent to that address into our inbox, and our email provider stores it there.
  • How long we keep it. Up to 12 months, then we delete it.
  • Your choice. Ask us to delete your email at any time. Write to quill@geodeforge.com and we will.

Two smaller things

  • Downloads. The files come from this site, so our host sees the download like any page view.
  • Fonts are your device's own. We don't load web fonts, because that would be a request to someone else.
