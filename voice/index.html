Make my voice

Paste a few things you wrote. GeodeQuill measures how you write, right here in your browser, and gives you a voice file your AI app can use. Your text never leaves this device.

1. Your samples

Three or more pieces, about 600 words in all or more: an email, a post, a letter. Put a line with three dashes (---) between pieces. Only text you wrote yourself; anything a tool helped write teaches the voice the wrong habits.

Transcripts of you talking: a speech, a voice note, an interview. Only your own words, without names, times or questions from others. Separate pieces with a line of three dashes. These let the voice sound right when it writes something to be said aloud.

Short, like "work" or "casual".

2. What it found

Phrases you repeat in your writing

Tick up to five that sound like you. They go into the voice word for word.

Each must appear exactly like that in your writing, and be under twelve words.

Phrases you repeat when you talk

Tick up to five that sound like you.

Each must appear exactly like that in what you said, and be under twelve words.

3. About you (optional)

Your own words go into the voice as you type them. Leave any of these empty.

For example: blunt or gentle, formal or casual, dry, warm or playful, how strongly you state opinions.

For example: no bullet points, no emoji, exclamation marks only with friends.

4. Your voice file

Using it

In ChatGPT, in Claude on the web, and in phone apps, attach or paste the file at the start of any chat where you want your voice, then ask for "write this in my voice".

On a computer with GeodeQuill installed, save the file in the folder .geodequill/voices inside your home folder. Every AI app on that computer that has GeodeQuill installed can then use it.

GeodeQuill can also make a voice for you inside your AI app: say "set up my voice". It then writes a test piece and adjusts the voice from what you tell it.

What it measures

Sentence and paragraph length and how much they vary, contractions, punctuation, how sentences start, reading level (for writing in the Latin alphabet, such as English or Spanish), and, for speech, fillers and questions. The phrases and words in the file are the ones you choose. Each measure becomes a range from how your own pieces differ from each other. GeodeQuill writes to those ranges and checks its drafts against them.

Your text stays here

The measuring runs inside your browser. What you paste is never sent anywhere, the page loads its files only from this site, and nothing is kept after you close the page unless you save the file. The first time downloads the measuring engine, about 12 MB; your browser keeps it after that.

The engine is Pyodide, which runs Python in a web page, used under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The measuring is GeodeQuill's own checker, and you can read every line.
